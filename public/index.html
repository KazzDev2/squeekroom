    How watts work

    Every settled Squeek block pays every room 10 W times your wheel multiplier, whether you are here or not. Squeektoshi runs when he feels like it, mostly late at night (UTC), and naps the rest of the day. Keeping this page open adds a small watching bonus. Wheel and bed upgrades are the only things that change how much you earn; everything else is for the room.

    Bed income: once no block has settled for 10 minutes he counts as asleep, and every room earns 1.5 W a minute times its bed multiplier (plus the watching bonus), online or not. It is much slower than the wheel, but it never stops while he naps.

    Squeekroom Watts come from settled Squeek blocks (SqueekCore on Sepolia), online or not. The hamster runs on his own schedule. Nothing here asks him to.
