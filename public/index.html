    How watts work

    Each Squeek block pays every room Watts. When Squeektoshi is asleep, you earn at roughly half the rate. Keep this tab open for +20%.

    Upgrade your wheel & bed to increase Watts production.

    Wager your Watts to raid and rob from dens that are at least 0.7x your balance.

    A failed raid burns the whole wager.

    Raiding becomes more +EV as you raid a much richer player.

    Being raided is always -EV, being high up on the leaderboard makes you a juicy target.

    Upgrade your den to reduce the chances of being raided successfully.
