How watts work

Every settled Squeek block pays every room 10 W times your wheel multiplier, whether you are here or not. Squeektoshi runs when he feels like it, mostly late at night (UTC), and naps the rest of the day. Keeping this page open adds a small watching bonus. Wheel upgrades are the only things that change how much you earn; everything else is for the room.

Squeekroom Watts come from settled Squeek blocks (SqueekCore on Sepolia), online or not. The hamster runs on his own schedule. Nothing here asks him to.
